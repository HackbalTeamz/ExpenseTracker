Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Expense Tracker is an offline personal finance app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is stored.

Information you enter

Transactions, opening balances, payment reminders, and the local username you create are stored on your device. The app uses a local database for financial records and Android secure storage for login credentials. The app does not send this information to us or to an app server.

Backups and exports

You can export or share backups and spreadsheets using Android's file picker and sharing features. Those files may contain your financial records; they are handled by the destination you choose and may be stored outside your device. Backup files do not include your login credentials. Keep exported files private.

Data retention and deletion

Your records remain on your device until you delete them in the app, restore over them, or uninstall the app. Android secure storage credentials are also kept on the device. Keep a backup before uninstalling if you want to retain your records.

Third-party services and tracking

Expense Tracker does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not require an online account. The app does not use location, contacts, camera, or microphone data.

Children's privacy

This app is a general personal finance utility and is not designed specifically for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated here and the date above will be revised.
